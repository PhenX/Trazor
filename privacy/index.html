Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-01

Trazor is built to need no trust: your images are converted entirely on your own device. Nothing you open is uploaded to any server, and Trazor has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising or tracking.

The studio (trazor.studio)

  • Every image you drop, paste, open, or receive is decoded, quantized, and traced in your browser. The image data never leaves your device.
  • Your settings and preferences are stored locally in your browser (localStorage). Recently opened images may be kept on your device (in IndexedDB) so you can reopen them; they are never sent anywhere, and a “Clear” action removes them.
  • Optional on-device machine-learning features may download model files (static assets). These are ordinary file downloads; no image or personal data is sent as part of them.
  • Trazor sets no advertising or tracking cookies and uses no third-party analytics.

The browser extension

  • The “Convert to SVG with Trazor” extension reads an image only when you right-click it and choose to convert. It uses that access solely to find the image you clicked (an image, a CSS background, an inline SVG, or a canvas).
  • It then hands those image bytes to the Trazor studio tab it opens, where they are traced on your device. The extension does not collect, store off your device, sell, or transmit any personal or usage data, and it contains no analytics or tracking.

Data we collect

None. Trazor does not collect or receive personal information or the contents of your images. Because processing is entirely client-side, there is no server that could see them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “last updated” date above will change. Material changes will be reflected here.
