Gradient fills — before / after

Smooth color ramps normally posterize into a stack of flat bands. With gradient detection on, adjacent bands that lie on one Oklab ramp — linear or radial — are merged into a single region painted with a standard <linearGradient> or <radialGradient> — mesh-free, so the geometry is unchanged: fewer shapes, smaller files, no banding. Flat art with no ramp is left exactly as before.
