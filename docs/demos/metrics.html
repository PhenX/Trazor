One scene, four metrics

The same synthetic source (a teal disc and an amber square) is compared against four reconstructions. Each metric answers a different question: mean dE asks "are the colors right?", SSIM asks "does the structure match?", Hausdorff asks "how far is the worst-placed edge?", and boundary IoU asks "do the edges agree within a small tolerance?". A shifted image is nearly perfect on dE but bad on Hausdorff; a posterized image is exactly the reverse — which is why the eval harnesses and the tune scorer report all of them, not one.
