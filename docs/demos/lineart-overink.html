Line-art scan: over-inked B&W vs. grayscale

A faint technical-drawing scan carries real gray tone — light construction lines, mid-gray hatching, halos around each stroke. Traced as black & white a threshold turns all of it solid ink: thin lines thicken and the hatched panel floods to a black mass. The recommender routes an achromatic line-art scan that is not cleanly two-tone to grayscale tonal layers, which keep each stroke at its true darkness. Measured over a corpus of real scanned drawings, this cuts mean Oklab ΔE ≈ 19% and spurious-hue ≈ 59%. The auto pick is outlined.
