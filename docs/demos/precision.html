Coordinate Precision — One Decimal vs Two

Every scene is traced twice with the studio's auto-recommended settings; only precision differs — two decimals in the path data on the left, one (the default) on the right. Rounding to one decimal moves a coordinate by at most 0.05 px, which no renderer resolves at 1× and which the crops below show at 6×; what changes is the file. Primitives are the <circle>, <ellipse> and <rect> elements recognized, arcs the A commands; both are fitted on the precision grid, so a coarser grid can change how many arcs a boundary keeps. The difference panel paints |two − one| amplified ×8, and the outlines overlay both paths (rust: two decimals, teal: one) on the one-decimal render.
