Trazor

Turn any image into clean, editable SVG — right in your browser

Trazor is a free, open-source raster-to-vector converter. Drop a PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF, BMP, AVIF or SVG and get crisp, editable, cut-ready SVG in seconds — with color, grayscale, black & white and centerline (single-stroke) tracing. Everything runs locally on your device, so your images are never uploaded.

  • 100% in your browser — no upload, no account, works offline
  • Color, grayscale, black & white and centerline tracing
  • Cut-ready output profiles for vinyl cutting, screen printing, laser engraving, pen plotters and stencils
  • Smooth Bézier curves, seam-free cutout layers and data-derived palettes
  • Auto-optimize settings and optional on-device ML tools
